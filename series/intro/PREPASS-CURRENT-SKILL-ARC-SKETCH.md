# Arc Sketch — Scrolltown weekly intro, 15-second prepass (current skill)

Step 0 of artist-dispatch: this sketch exists before any drawing.
It is the shared guiding light for every packet in this dispatch.
Sequence: the ruled 15-second weekly intro (Will, 2026-11-21 —
Fern and Waverly featured; character-led, not a world-only sigil).
Task 24.7.8/9.7.11/7.8.12. Mode: PREPASS — the whole arc at low
fidelity so the arc is judged whole. Picture only, silent; the
audio window is still Will's open call and no window is chosen here.

Format marks: 854x480, 24 fps, 360 frames, exactly 15.000 s.
Frame 0 is the first frame. Beats are frame ranges, cuts on the
beat line, four-frame warm dips between beats.

## Beat 1 — Fern lifts her lantern (frames 0-95, 0.0-4.0 s)

Key pose A (frame 0): Fern stands left of center on Home Street at
dusk, lantern held low at her side, flame small. Houses behind her
are dark. She is about two fifths of the frame height — her anchors
read at thumbnail size.
Key pose B (frame 95): the same stance, arm raised, lantern high,
flame full, a pool of warm light at her feet, the nearest window
answering. Timing: the lift is one eased arc, slow in and slow out,
frames cluster at both ends; a held breath of stillness (frames
8-20) before the arm moves — anticipation as a held beat, never a
crouch. Camera: static, wide enough that she is never chased.

## Beat 2 — Waverly unrolls the Great Scroll (frames 96-215, 4.0-9.0 s)

Key pose C (frame 96): Waverly stands right of center, the Great
Scroll a tight roll between her hands; Fern, smaller, at the left
edge, lantern still raised toward her.
Key pose D (frame 215): the scroll open wide between them, a town
drawn on its page in ink, and the drawn town's windows lit house by
house — ten houses, in reading order, none going dark once lit.
Timing: the unroll eases open across frames 110-170; the drawn
windows light one per six frames from frame 150. Waverly's anchors:
plum coat, long dark hair, one gold streak. Camera: static.

## Beat 3 — Pull wide, the real town answers (frames 216-311, 9.0-13.0 s)

Key pose E (frame 216): the beat-2 framing, held one breath.
Key pose F (frame 311): the whole town wide — Home Street, the
tower, the river bend — Fern and Waverly small at center, and the
real windows answering the drawn ones in a seeded random order,
wave on wave, until the town hums gold. Timing: the pull is one
eased move, frames 224-280, scale from full to just under half;
the answering lights run the whole beat and complete by frame 300.
Camera: the pull IS the camera move, drawn, not generated.

## Beat 4 — Title over the tiling field (frames 312-359, 13.0-15.0 s)

Key pose G (frame 359): the woven tiling field complete — a diamond
grid of squares, five diagonal lines in each, orientations
alternating in the weave, drawn fast in Will's manner — dimmed
under the word SCROLLTOWN lettered in the Emise font, ballpoint
blue, over a bottom strip of the lit town. Timing: the field's
lines land in the first twenty frames, staggered at random; the
letters arrive one per three frames from frame 324; the last two
seconds hold. Nothing that lit goes dark, in any beat.

## Register and anchors (the canon this prepass is judged against)

Drawn route only, Emise design language: the flowing sharp line of
the Emise font baseline, fast-hand stroke character with jitter and
overshoot kept, hatching in Will's manner; a page passes the feel
test when it feels Will-to-Emise touched. Fern's anchors, drawn the
same way on every page by construction: twin scroll-puff buns,
butter-yellow scarf bow, brass key-badge on its dark cord. Waverly's
anchors: deep-plum coat, one gold streak in long dark hair.
Identity is held by construction and then enumerated, never assumed.
