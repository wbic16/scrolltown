## Vocal direction

- Lead: warm adult male voice, singing like he's telling a child a true story — clear, kind, unhurried. Not a rock belt; a narrator who sings.
- Verses 1–3: lead alone, orchestra intimate (strings + piano + harp).
- Bridge: lead + soft humming choir underneath.
- Builds A–B: orchestra grows section by section — more strings, horns enter, light percussion like a heartbeat. **Nothing that enters ever drops out again** (the arrangement obeys the craft law: ignitions accumulate).
- Chorus: lead + children's choir on the hook ("Light the next light…"), full orchestra, French horns carrying the Voyager swell.
- Refrain (2100): pull back to lead + children's voices + strings; final line almost a whisper that opens into one last warm chord that does **not** resolve home — it leans forward, into 2130, which the intro does not depict.
