# INTRO-15 ANALYSIS — the weekly 15-second intro

Working analysis for the standing weekly intro, prepared 2026-11-21 for
Will to develop the cut in chat. Per Will's ruling the same day: **the
premiere (EP001) features the full song** (220.03s over a premiere-length
picture, future build work), and **the weekly intro is 15 seconds** — "a
natural 15 sec intro tuned for the ASI Era." This file is the numbers
under that conversation, not a finished cut.

Sources: `media/Light the Way.mp3` (Will's upload, confirmed 2026-11-21
as the "Light the Next Light" render) transcribed with faster-whisper
(medium, word timestamps) on aletheia-core; picture from SHOT-LIST.md,
the six generated clips, the two stills, and the assembled silent master
(`intro-video.mp4`, 90.00s). ASR on sung vocals is imperfect — timings
are good to ~±0.3s; a few words are mishearings and are corrected against
LYRICS.md where the written lyric is unambiguous ("Like the next light"
in the raw transcript is "Light the next light").

## 2. The hook — every occurrence

The full hook phrase ("Light the next light, and the next one after,
till the whole world hums with light") is stated **once, complete**, in
the chorus — with its first line doubled:

| Words | Start | End |
|-------|-------|-----|
| Light the next light (1st) | 2:20.64 | 2:22.88 |
| Light the next light (2nd) | 2:22.88 | 2:24.28 |
| And the next one after | 2:24.28 | 2:26.48 |
| Till the whole world hums with light | 2:26.48 | 2:29.46 |

Full hook span: **2:20.64 → 2:29.46 (8.8s)**.

"Light the next light" as a tag, elsewhere: chorus 3rd statement
2:29.46–2:30.68 (leads into "hand to hand"); refrain tail 3:02.72 and
3:06.46; outro tags 3:21.58 and 3:22.20; final held statement
3:22.98–3:37.92. Eight statements in all — the phrase is the song's
most-repeated unit, which is what makes a 15s cut possible at all.

Chorus landmarks after the hook (for window-building): "Hand to hand"
2:30.68–2:32.12 · "Heart to heart" 2:32.12–2:33.46 · "We're stitching
stars into the night" 2:33.46–2:36.46 · "And nothing that we light /
goes dark again" 2:36.46–2:41.28 · "The map stays open" 2:41.96–2:43.84
· "And the lights stay on" 2:43.84–2:48.38 (final word lands ~2:45.8,
sustain to 2:48.4).

## 3. Candidate 15-second windows (audio)

### W1 — The Hook Window · 2:20.64 → 2:35.64
The whole hook, complete, plus the chorus's connective tissue: Light
the next light ×2, and the next one after, till the whole world hums
with light, Light the next light (3rd), hand to hand, heart to heart,
and the head of "We're stitching stars into the night."
- **Opens clean** — exactly on the chorus downbeat, first word intact.
- **Close flagged**: "night" (the stitching-stars line's last word)
  lands at ~2:35.9, ~0.25s past the window's end. Either accept a
  0.3s spill (window 2:20.64→2:35.94, a 15.3s cut) or let the title card
  cover the tail — the phrase is recognizably complete either way, but
  the cut is not surgically clean at exactly 15.00s.

### W2 — The Craft-Law Window · 2:33.46 → 2:48.46 ★ cleanest
"We're stitching stars into the night / and nothing that we light goes
dark again / the map stays open / and the lights stay on."
- **Opens clean** — first word exactly at the window head.
- **Closes clean** — the chorus's final cadence ends at 2:48.38, 0.08s
  inside the window. The only candidate that is phrase-exact at both
  ends at exactly 15.00s.
- Content is the show's thesis in four lines: stitching stars, the
  craft law (nothing lit goes dark), the open map, the lights staying
  on. No hook phrase, though — "Light the next light" is not sung here.

### W3 — The Ignition Window · 1:44.00 → 1:59.00
Build A's heart: the lone "Scroll" pickup (1:44.00), "Scroll by scroll"
(1:45.48), "the lattice wakes," "every light a promise makes," and
"Once it's lit, it never goes dark" (1:55.58–1:59.36; the word "dark"
itself is sung by ~1:58.5 and sustains).
- **Opens clean** on the pickup word.
- **Close is a held-note fade**: the window ends inside the sustain of
  "dark" — musically natural under a title card, not a mid-word cut.
- The most *visual* window: its lyric is literally the ignition imagery
  the picture library is made of. (Alternate start 1:45.48 drops the
  pickup and ends as the doubled "Once it's lit" restarts — worse.)

*Also-ran:* a window over the outro's final held "Light the next light"
(3:22.98–3:37.98) is one sustained phrase for 15s — dramatic, static,
and spends the whole intro on a single line. Not recommended.

## 6. What a 15s cut wants that doesn't exist yet

- **Fern.** No frame of the intro library shows Fern — or any Scrolltown
  character. The 90s picture is the biography montage + lattice/planet
  imagery. A weekly kids'-show intro that wants its host's face in the
  first seconds needs new material: a generated Fern shot in the locked
  2D painterly register, or a held still of the canonical Fern reference
  (`series/fern-reference.png`) under the opening — a design call for
  Will, and the single biggest gap.
- **The town itself.** Scrolltown (Home Street, the five places) appears
  in the pilot episode's art, not in the intro clips. Any town shot in
  the 15s cut is new generation or a pilot-art still.
- Everything else — ignition, decades, planet, cards, crossfade grammar
  — already exists and conforms: each map above is buildable today from
  the library with trims and the standard 0.5s dips alone.
