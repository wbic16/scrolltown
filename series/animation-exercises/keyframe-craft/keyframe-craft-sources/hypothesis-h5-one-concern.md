# Hypothesis H5 — one concern per packet, worked deeply

Claim: the artists handle key frames well because each packet carries
a single concern over a narrow span and works it to the bottom,
while a generated clip asks one sampling process to hold identity,
camera, staging, physics, and register across hundreds of frames at
once — and fails exactly at the joints between those concerns.

Evidence walked:
- Velum's packet (27.6.9/8.6.11/7.6.9) covers one clip in two
  operations: 60 frames measured, 180 frames enumerated. Nothing in
  it trades one clause against another.
- Theia's Gate 1 work (30.7.9/8.7.13/9.6.9) is four packets, one
  mechanical operation each — acquire, survey, extract, assemble.
- Alveus's packet is four stills, each with its own named likeness
  check (22.6.9/7.6.10/7.6.8).
- The generated failures are joint failures: identity lost where
  body motion was asked (24.7.9/8.7.11/7.6.8, 25.8.8/9.7.12/7.6.9);
  the landing invented where the camera was free
  (25.8.8/9.7.12/7.6.9, 25.7.10/8.6.10/7.6.9); a scene re-staged
  where two keys had to be honored at once (25.7.10/8.6.10/7.6.9).
- The control pair inside the generator record: the pendulum —
  single concern, face-free — passed (25.7.10/8.6.10/7.6.9); the
  bouncing ball — equally single-concern but demanding object
  permanence from sampling — failed four times. Narrowness helps
  only when the route can hold the one concern (see H2).

Verdict: SUPPORTED, jointly with H2: decomposition is the shape of
the work; determinism is what makes the decomposed pieces hold.
