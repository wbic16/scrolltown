# Hypothesis H3 — per-frame addressing lets enumeration replace sampling

Claim: the artists handle key frames well because every frame is
addressable, so their effort — and QC's attention — lands on exactly
the frames in question, enumerated, instead of on samples from which
the whole is guessed.

Evidence walked:
- Batch 2.1's anchor verification (18.13.15/9.12.15/13.14.16) reads
  all 180 frames of its range individually: five identity anchors at
  180/180, blinks noted as motion rather than loss. The programme's
  identity-at-length evidence was upgraded by this return from
  sampled to enumerated (25.8.8/9.7.12/7.6.9).
- The same packet's measurement pair (45.12.11/13.14.14/12.13.15 and
  39.23.21/18.25.19/15.15.18) replaced Burn 2's impression of
  "reframing" with a number: translation dx = 0 on all 60 frames,
  |dy| ≤ 2 px, scale 1.00 → 1.04 — a ≈4 % zoom, not a drift.
- The pendulum's spacing clause (25.7.10/8.6.10/7.6.9) was settled
  only when attention went per-frame: a 40-position chart transcribed
  frame by frame, after three automated samplers failed.
- The gate validator (26.8.9/10.7.12/7.7.8) scores every page against
  its matched original and found what sampled QC had missed
  entirely: one title card never drawn, and the wash defect present
  on all 60 pages where sampling had reported "softening".

Verdict: SUPPORTED. Addressing is what makes enumeration cheap
enough to actually do; every time the programme moved from sampling
to enumeration, a soft claim became an exact one.
