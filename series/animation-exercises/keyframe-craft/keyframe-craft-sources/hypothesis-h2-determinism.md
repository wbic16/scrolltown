# Hypothesis H2 — determinism and reference-lock beat temporal sampling

Claim: the artists' work succeeds because the operations they execute
are deterministic or locked to a reference — the same input yields the
same output, and any defect is systematic — while Emise's failed key
frames came from open-ended generative sampling across time, which
invents something new on every attempt.

Evidence walked:
- Theia's Gate 1 operations (30.7.9/8.7.13/9.6.9) are pipeline
  mechanics: a download verified byte-exact by sha256, scene-boundary
  detection at a stated threshold (79 boundaries), one extracted frame
  per shot (60 pages), assembly at stated shot timing. Each is
  reproducible by construction.
- Velum's Batch 2.1 operations (27.6.9/8.6.11/7.6.9) are measurement
  and conform: phase correlation per frame (45.12.11/13.14.14/12.13.15),
  a best-fit scale table (39.23.21/18.25.19/15.15.18), a counter-crop
  that is the originals to within 2 px. Numbers, not impressions.
- The drawn route is deterministic end to end: the Phext P4 tiling
  (27.6.9/7.7.11/7.6.8) runs a recorded seed and Will's verdict on it
  was "the animation is perfect". The Gate 1 repro pages come from a
  line renderer whose one real defect — a flat wash at luminance
  ≈ 205 with interior ink density 0.00 where solid black belongs —
  is identical on all 60 pages (26.8.9/10.7.12/7.7.8): systematic,
  therefore mechanically detectable and fixable in one place.
- The generator's failures are the mirror image — a different
  invention every attempt: a photoreal face morph and hair re-drawn
  into curls (23.7.9/8.7.10/6.6.9); a crouch promoted to the main
  action (24.7.9/8.7.11/7.6.8); an invented unrolled scroll covered in
  pseudo-writing (25.8.8/9.7.12/7.6.9); a token duplicated, then
  morphed loaf → dome → ring → oval, a scene re-staged around two
  supplied keys, a character walking to camera wearing invented
  jewelry (25.7.10/8.6.10/7.6.9). None is reproducible; none can be
  corrected by fixing one parameter.
- The one generative artist task, Batch 2.2's variant stills
  (22.6.9/7.6.10/7.6.8), succeeded precisely where the temporal
  dimension is absent: single frames, seeded from the canonical
  reference, judged against a named likeness check — draft grade,
  with staging notes. Generation held a still; it has never held
  a sequence.

Verdict: SUPPORTED — the primary mechanism. Refinement the evidence
forces: the operative variable is the route an operation runs on,
not the worker who runs it. Deterministic or reference-locked work
succeeds whoever executes it; temporal sampling fails whoever asks
for it.
