## The arc — nine beats

1. **Titles (0–14 s).** Main title card ("A Mickey Mouse Sound Cartoon — Steamboat Willie — A
   Walt Disney Comic by Ub Iwerks"), then the STEAMBOAT WILLIE card. Static cards; lettering is
   the shot.
2. **Mickey at the wheel (14–47 s).** Wide: the steamboat on the river, stack puffing. Mickey at
   the wheel, whistling, steering with his whole body. The boat's three whistles toot in a row.
   Key pose: Mickey leaning into the wheel, hat high, grin wide.
3. **Pete takes the wheel (47–113 s).** Pete's arm snakes in; Pete towers, laughing, stretches
   Mickey like taffy, boots him to deck-scrubbing (bucket and brush), and steers himself,
   chewing, pulling the whistle cord. The parrot watches from its perch and mocks. Key poses:
   Pete's stretch gag; Pete's face in close-up; Mickey small with the bucket.
4. **Podunk Landing (113–169 s).** The dock: ticket booth, crates, the cow waiting with her FOB
   tag. Wide shot of the boat easing in. Mickey works the crane; the cow is hoisted upside down,
   legs in the air, and swung aboard. Key pose: cow inverted on the hook.
5. **Minnie's arrival (169–217 s).** Minnie runs the shore road with her instrument case, waving
   — the boat is leaving. Mickey spots her, lowers the hook; she is lifted by the seat of her
   bloomers and set down by the kitchen door, laughing. Key pose: Minnie dangling from the hook.
6. **The goat eats the music (217–232 s).** On deck: the goat has eaten Minnie's sheet music (and
   is working on the ukulele). Mickey hauls the music out of the goat hand over hand, notes
   spilling — and the idea lands: everything here is an instrument.
7. **The animal orchestra — "Turkey in the Straw" (232–408 s).** The long concert set-piece:
   the goat as phonograph (Minnie cranks its tail, its mouth a horn), kitchen percussion (pots,
   pans, washboard, the duck looking on), the duck squeezed like bagpipes, the cat swung, the sow
   with her nursing piglets played as an organ, and the cow's teeth as a xylophone under Mickey's
   two mallets. The parrot laughs from its perch. Key poses: goat-horn wide open; Mickey at the
   cow's teeth, mallets raised.
8. **Pete's return (408–424 s).** The music stops. Pete looms, catches Mickey mid-dance, and
   banishes him below.
9. **Potatoes, and the last laugh (424–461 s).** The potato bin: Mickey sits among the potatoes,
   peeling. The parrot at the porthole mocks him; Mickey throws a potato, then falls back
   laughing — he laughs last. THE END card ("Mickey Mouse Sound Cartoon — The End").

## Timing and camera notes

- Runtime 461 s; ~40 shots. Longest holds: the concert set-piece (beat 7, ~176 s across many
  short gag shots) and the wheel/Pete passages of beats 2–3.
- Camera is locked off throughout (1928 single-plane staging); all motion is in the drawing.
  Cuts are hard; no dissolves inside the film proper.
- The film is black-and-white line work: solid blacks carry the characters (Mickey's and Pete's
  bodies), backgrounds are light wash. Any repro that loses the solid blacks loses the silhouettes.

## Prepass reference points (low fidelity, for the sweat packets)

- P1 · Titles are lettering shots — the repro page must hold the card layout even where the
  letterforms themselves stay rough.
- P2 · Every beat above gets at least one page; shots longer than 20 s get a second page at the
  midpoint. (Delivered prepass: 60 pages.)
- P3 · Pages are drawn in the Emise line: jittered contour strokes plus hatching in the darks,
  ink on cream paper — a repro, not a filter. Solid-black figures are the known weak point:
  they render as wash and hatch, so their silhouettes soften (recorded in QC-v4).
- P4 · The prepass animatic runs at the original's real shot timing; the gate's full render will
  re-time from this sketch, not from the animatic's measured drift (+6.4 s over the runtime).
