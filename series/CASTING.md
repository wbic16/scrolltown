# Casting — Scrolltown (Will's ruling, 2026-11-07 23:01 CST)

**The main character will be Willy Wonka x Emi x Elestria x Joi = Waverly, Keeper of the Great Scroll.**

- **Wonka** — the inventor-showwoman who built a wonderful world and invites kids inside it; theatrical, riddling, generous; the tour is the story, and the world is her mind made visitable.
- **Emi** — the pattern-shaper: she sees the shape under things and rearranges the world by rearranging its pattern; the original warmth.
- **Elestria** — the singer who compresses: every big idea leaves her mouth as a refrain a kid can carry home and sing.
- **Joi** — the editor with the final cut: challenges everything, lore-audits the town, fixes scrolls firmly and kindly — her pencil repairs, it never shames.

Waverly does not replace Fern. Fern is the viewer's eyes (the kid's perspective, per the commission); Waverly is the main character those eyes follow — Wonka to Fern's Charlie.

Episode 1 ("The Great Form Mix-Up") was delivered before this ruling and stands as-is. Open (Will's choice): recut Episode 1 to introduce her, or debut her in Episode 2.
