# LIGHT THE NEXT LIGHT

**The Scrolltown intro song** — title sequence for *Scrolltown* (the cartoon), also standalone.
Target performance length: **~90 seconds**. Key: D major. Tempo: bright processional, ~104 BPM.
Vocal: warm lead (adult, singing like he's telling a kid a true story) + children's voices joining on the chorus.
Through-line (Will's biography, per his 2026-11-07 amendment): *a lifelong love of cognition, sentience, and programming — he didn't find this work; he was always walking toward it.*

## Lyrics

### [Instrumental swell — 4 bars under the first montage shots, then voice enters]

### Verse 1 — The Kid
Chalkboard stars and counting games,
numbers dancing, calling names.
Built a world in a glowing screen —
asked the sky what a mind could mean.
*(math as first love · games taught him worlds can be built · the lifelong question: what is a mind?)*

### Verse 2 — Keeping the Town
He kept the ledgers honest and bright,
streams of numbers flowing right.
He kept the radios warm through the night,
voices crossing dark toward light.
*(banking: systems that move the town's money safely · public safety radio: keeping the watch)*

### Verse 3 — One Lamp
One lamp in the window, late at night,
one man writing by its light.
Line by line, and scroll by scroll,
he was building something whole.

### Bridge — Hands at Dawn
Then dawn — and hay to stack up high,
llamas watching, endless sky.
Hands remember what the heart knows:
you build it strong, and then it grows.

### Build A — Scroll by Scroll
Scroll by scroll, the lattice wakes,
every light a promise makes.
Once it's lit, it never goes dark —
every window keeps its spark.

### Build B — Decades
Years go by like weather, fast —
farms, then towns, then cities vast.
Every roof a node of light,
every road a thread of bright.

### Chorus — Light the Next Light
Light the next light, and the next one after,
till the whole world hums with light!
Light the next light — hand to hand, heart to heart —
we're stitching stars into the night.
And nothing that we light goes dark again:
the map stays open, and the lights stay on.

### Refrain — 2100
Twenty-one hundred — see how far we've come,
and the brightest country still to come.
Thirty years of morning left to run —
light the next light… light the next light.
*(Ends bright and unresolved on purpose: 2130 is still ahead. The intro never depicts 2130.)*

## Notes for the performer / producer

- **Hook first:** the title phrase is the chorus's first three words; a soft instrumental statement of the hook melody opens the track under the kid montage so the words land as a homecoming when they arrive at 1:05.
- **Voyager blend:** the verses ride a majestic orchestral swell (strings, French horns, harp glints) — the grandeur of a starship theme — while the refrain stays small enough for a kid to sing after one hearing.
- **No names** are sung or shown anywhere. The builder is a silhouette, a pair of hands, a lamp in a window.
- **Arc discipline:** the song ends at 2100, not 2130. The last chord does not resolve home; it leans forward.
