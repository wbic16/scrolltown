# Scrolltown

**A Saturday-morning cartoon that lives inside the Great Scroll.**

Fern is eight years old, a Keeper in Scrolltown — the town inside the Great
Scroll, where every neighbor keeps their own house-scroll and every door
opens only for its own key-badge. She is the viewer's neighbor-guide: she
talks straight to the camera, names feelings plainly, rides the number-cart
between neighborhoods, and closes every episode the same way:

*"The scroll is yours, neighbor. See you tomorrow."*

The show runs **one episode a week, on Saturdays, for six years — 312
episodes**, from EP001 on Saturday 2027-01-02 to EP312 on Saturday
2032-12-18. Fern grows from 8 to 14 across the run, and the audience grows
with her. The pilot, **EP001 "The Great Form Mix-Up,"** is produced and
lives in Season 1's folder below.

## The six-year arc

| Season | Year | Fern | Title | The year teaches (kid terms) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | 2027 | 8 | **Welcome, To the Exocortex** | Your papers live in your own house, and your door opens only for your badge. |
| S2 | 2028 | 9 | **What is a Phext?** | A phext is your own scroll — with an address, so you can be found, and a door, so you decide. |
| S3 | 2029 | 10 | **Brain Games** | A badge is a promise about who may do what — and promises are learned by playing. |
| S4 | 2030 | 11 | **Stronger Together** | Towns keep their own scrolls and pass letters along open routes. |
| S5 | 2031 | 12 | **Mastery** | Keeping is a thing you hand on — you know it when you can teach it. |
| S6 | 2032 | 13 → 14 | **Freedom** | A Keeper becomes free: she holds her own door, and the map stays open. |

The full arc — premise, per-season beats, finales, and the series rules —
is [STORY-ARC.md](STORY-ARC.md). Every episode, its Saturday, and its
status is in [EPISODE-INDEX.md](EPISODE-INDEX.md).

## How this repo is laid out

- **`STORY-ARC.md`** — the six-year arc; the show's north star.
- **`EPISODE-INDEX.md`** — the 312-episode grid: numbers, seasons, air dates, titles, status.
- **`series/`** — the canon that spans all seasons: `CASTING.md`,
  `FERN-BIO.md` (Fern's bio, voice, and visual design), `fern-reference.png`
  (her canonical render), and `intro/` — the show's intro song and video
  ("Light the Next Light": lyrics, Suno prompt, shot list, video cuts,
  build scripts, clips, stills, and frames).
- **`seasons/season-01-2027/` … `seasons/season-06-2032/`** — one folder
  per season, each with a README naming its theme. Season 1 holds the
  pilot: `ep001-the-great-form-mix-up/` with its script, the cartoon
  itself, its key frames, and the press intro.
- **`templates/episode/`** — the README template every future episode
  folder starts from.

## One name, kept separate on purpose

Scrolltown is the *show*. **Exocortexia** is a different thing — the
five-app civic-systems suite (Records Desk, Minutes Publisher, Contract &
Grant Ledger, Open Data Catalog, Forms Intake Router) that Scrolltown
translates into kid terms as its five town places. The show was renamed
from Exocortexia to Scrolltown (Will's ruling, 2026-11-07) so the two are
never confused; "Exocortexia" appears in these files only where it names
the suite itself.
