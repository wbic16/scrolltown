# Scrolltown

Scrolltown is a cartoon from a kid's perspective: a town inside the Great Scroll, where every citizen keeps their own house-scroll and the doors open only for their owner. Fern — eight years old, a Keeper — narrates.

**Episode 1: "Scrolltown: The Great Form Mix-Up"** — the Post Tower sneezes, two papers land in the wrong houses, and Fern's crew puts them right.

## What's here

- `SCRIPT.md` — Episode 1 script & treatment
- `CASTING.md` — cast sheet and casting rulings
- `FERN-BIO.md` — Fern's character bio
- `FERN-KEYFRAMES-MJ.md` — Midjourney prompts for the key frames
- `INTRO-FOR-JAEL.md` — a three-line intro to Fern and Scrolltown, written for Jael
- `cartoon.html` — Episode 1 as a single self-contained playable web cartoon
- `fern-reference.png` — the canonical Fern render (the design of record)
- `keyframes/` — Midjourney key frames for Episode 1
- `intro/` — "Light the Next Light," the intro song & video: `LYRICS.md`, `SUNO-PROMPT.md`, `SHOT-LIST.md`, the video in master / small / guide cuts, and the `build/`, `clips/`, `stills/`, and `frames/` it was made from

Exocortexia is a separate project — the civic-systems suite. Scrolltown is the show; the two names stay separate on purpose.
