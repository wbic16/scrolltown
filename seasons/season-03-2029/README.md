# Season 3 · 2029 · "Brain Games"

**Fern is 10.**

The practice season, played as games. Keeping becomes something the whole town rehearses together — and Waverly, Keeper of the Great Scroll, steps forward as mentor-presence.

**The year teaches (kid terms):** A badge is a promise about who may do what — and promises are learned by playing.

**Tentpole:** "The Mirror Maze" — Games of memory, inference, and prediction teach Fern that intelligence and being correct are different things.
