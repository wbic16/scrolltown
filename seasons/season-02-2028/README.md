# Season 2 · 2028 · "What is a Phext?"

**Fern is 9.**

Fern asks what the Exocortex is made of. A phext becomes an idea she can say — my own scroll, at my own address, behind my own door — and the Scroll widens into neighborhoods to teach it.

**The year teaches (kid terms):** A phext is your own scroll — with an address, so you can be found, and a door, so you decide.

**Tentpole:** "The House Beyond the Map" — Fern learns to navigate the dimensions of Phext and discovers how much remains unexplored.
